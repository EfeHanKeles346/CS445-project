# Terms and Conditions

By registering for and participating in **SemEval-2027 Task 3: Semantic Change Across Multiple Time Periods**, participants agree to the following terms and conditions.

## 1. Eligibility

Participation is open to researchers, students, and practitioners from academia and industry worldwide.

Participants may compete individually or as part of a team.

---

## 2. Registration

Each team must register through the official Codabench platform.

Teams are responsible for ensuring that all submitted information is accurate and that contact information remains up to date throughout the competition.


---

## 3. Use of the Data

The datasets provided for this challenge are intended **exclusively for research purposes within the scope of this shared task**.

Participants agree to:

- use the data only for participation in this challenge;
- comply with all dataset licenses and copyright restrictions;
- not redistribute, publish, or share any part of the evaluation data;
- not attempt to reconstruct or infer hidden annotations.

The evaluation data may not be manually annotated, incorporated into future training data, or used to train, evaluate, or improve models unless explicitly permitted by the organizers after the competition.

---

## 4. External Resources

Unless otherwise specified in the task description, participants may use publicly available resources, including:

- pretrained language models;
- publicly available corpora;
- dictionaries and lexical resources;
- publicly released software libraries.

Any external resources used must be clearly documented in the system description paper.

---

## 5. Submissions

Participants may submit multiple runs during the evaluation period, subject to the submission limits specified on the challenge page.

Each submission must:

- follow the required file format;
- contain predictions generated automatically by the submitted system;
- not include manually created predictions;
- not intentionally exploit weaknesses in the evaluation software.

The organizers reserve the right to reject invalid or malformed submissions.

**Final Results and Ranking**
Teams may submit multiple solutions throughout the competition. For each team, only the submission achieving the highest score will be considered and included in the final report, regardless of the submission order.

---

## 6. Fair Participation

Participants agree not to:

- share evaluation data or hidden labels;
- attempt to reverse engineer the evaluation dataset;
- create multiple accounts;
- interfere with the operation of the Codabench platform;
- exploit bugs or vulnerabilities in the evaluation infrastructure.

Any violation may result in disqualification.

---

## 7. Leaderboard

Scores displayed on the public leaderboard are preliminary.

The organizers reserve the right to:

- recompute scores;
- rerun submissions;
- invalidate submissions that violate the competition rules;
- correct evaluation errors discovered during the competition.

The final rankings announced by the organizers shall be considered authoritative.

---

## 8. Publication

Participants wishing to be included in the official shared task proceedings must submit at least one valid submission to Evaluation Phase 1, Phase 2 or both within the time frame specified on the task page; and a system description paper detailing all steps taken and according to the SemEval guidelines no later than the deadline announced on the shared task page. 

Acceptance of a system description paper remains subject to the SemEval reviewing process.

Participation in the challenge does not guarantee publication. 

---
## 9. Reproducibility

Participants are strongly encouraged to make their code publicly available after the competition.

The organizers may request additional information regarding submitted systems in order to verify reported results.

Failure to provide sufficient information may result in exclusion from the official ranking.

---

## 10. Organizer Rights

The organizers reserve the right to:

- modify the competition schedule if necessary;

- correct errors in the datasets or evaluation software;

- update the documentation;

- remove submissions that violate these terms;

- cancel or suspend the competition in exceptional circumstances.

Any significant changes will be communicated through the official competition page and in the corresponding Google group mailing list. Participants are responsible for keeping updated. 

---

## 11. Privacy

Participant information collected during registration will be used solely for the administration of the shared task and related scientific activities.

Personal information will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection regulations.

---

## 12. Acceptance of the Terms

By registering for and participating in this challenge, participants acknowledge that they have read and agree to these Terms and Conditions.

If you have any questions regarding these rules, please contact the organizers before submitting your system.
